Source notes

This is a fan-made planning aid. Product/scenario structure and official encounter modules follow printed scenario setup cards and official preconstructed lists where available. The optional encounter tuner uses published VillainTheory combinations cataloged by Modular Champions and the source guide’s highest-rated recipes, with solo, two-player, and 3–4-player modular-set ratings from VillainTheory’s May 2026 matrix. Official setup always remains the default; unavailable alternatives are labeled rather than invented. Most portraits are cropped from Fantasy Flight Games’ Marvel Champions card illustrations; remaining characters use locally cached community reference art. No portrait requires a network connection. Hero recommendations start with the most-liked MarvelCDB deck that includes a substantial play guide. A qualifying deck published within the last 12 months is preferred only when it has at least 70% of the community leader’s likes. Recommendations were checked August 3, 2026; Luke Cage and Jessica Jones remain pending until MarvelCDB publishes decklists for them. Marvel characters and imagery belong to their respective rights holders.
