Source notes

This is a fan-made planning aid. Product/scenario structure and encounter modules follow printed scenario setup cards and official preconstructed lists where available. Fear No Evil entries distinguish confirmed campaign pairings from modular recommendations that are still absent from the public card catalog. Most portraits are cropped from Fantasy Flight Games’ Marvel Champions card illustrations; remaining characters use locally cached community reference art. No portrait requires a network connection. Hero recommendations start with the most-liked MarvelCDB deck that includes a substantial play guide. A qualifying deck published within the last 12 months is preferred only when it has at least 70% of the community leader’s likes. Recommendations were checked August 3, 2026; Luke Cage and Jessica Jones remain pending until MarvelCDB publishes decklists for them. Marvel characters and imagery belong to their respective rights holders.
