Source notes

This is a fan-made planning aid. Product/scenario structure is grounded in Fantasy Flight Games and Hall of Heroes. Most portraits are cropped from Fantasy Flight Games’ Marvel Champions card illustrations; remaining characters use locally cached community reference art. No portrait requires a network connection. Hero recommendations start with the most-liked MarvelCDB deck that includes a substantial play guide. A qualifying deck published within the last 12 months is preferred only when it has at least 70% of the community leader’s likes. Recommendations were checked August 3, 2026; Luke Cage and Jessica Jones remain pending until MarvelCDB publishes decklists for them. Marvel characters and imagery belong to their respective rights holders.
