# Marvel Champions — Thematic Two-Hero Matchups

**Scope:** 65 scenario matchups through Fear No Evil. Shadowland is excluded because it is upcoming in this sequence.

**Repeat marker:** 🔁 means the hero appeared earlier in the sequence.

**Release-order constraint:** Luke Cage and Jessica Jones are not used before Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.; they become eligible afterward.

## Core Set

### 1. Rhino
- **Villain side:** Rhino
- **Hero team:** Spider-Man (Peter Parker) + She-Hulk
- **Context:** Peter gets his classic street-level enemy, while Jen supplies the muscle to stop Rhino without turning the fight into a cosmic event.

### 2. Klaw
- **Villain side:** Klaw
- **Hero team:** Black Panther (T’Challa) + Storm
- **Context:** Klaw is fundamentally a Wakandan enemy; T’Challa brings the personal stakes while Storm’s history as Wakanda’s queen gives her a direct reason to defend it.

### 3. Ultron
- **Villain side:** Ultron
- **Hero team:** Ant-Man + Wasp
- **Context:** Both heroes are tied directly to the Pym legacy and therefore to Ultron’s creation and recurring attacks.

## Green Goblin

### 4. Risky Business — Norman Osborn / Green Goblin
- **Villain side:** Norman Osborn + Green Goblin
- **Hero team:** Spider-Man (Peter Parker) 🔁 + Ghost-Spider
- **Context:** Peter gets his defining Goblin feud, while Gwen understands better than almost anyone what a Goblin can cost a Spider-hero.

### 5. Mutagen Formula — Green Goblin
- **Villain side:** Green Goblin
- **Hero team:** Spider-Woman + Spider-Man (Miles Morales)
- **Context:** Jessica brings espionage and HYDRA connections; Miles represents the next Spider generation taking on Norman’s escalating madness.

## The Wrecking Crew

### 6. The Wrecking Crew
- **Villain side:** Wrecker + Piledriver + Bulldozer + Thunderball
- **Hero team:** Thor + Hercules
- **Context:** The Crew’s enchanted power belongs directly in Thor’s corner of Marvel, and Hercules turns the encounter into the proper god-tier bar fight it deserves.

## The Once and Future Kang
